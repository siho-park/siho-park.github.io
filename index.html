Health & Public Economics

Siho Park

Assistant Professor of Economics
KDI School of Public Policy and Management

I study what happens when people are offered a screening test, a subsidy, or a piece of information — and why so many decline, delay, or misread the signal. My research draws on national health-screening records and field data from Korea, the Philippines, and Indonesia to trace how beliefs and incentives shape health behavior, and who gets left out of preventive care.

  • Health screening & selection
  • Health behavior & misinformation
  • Gender, education & health
